Where Interfacer fits best

  • Your customers use more than one kind of system. If you need a CRM sync this quarter and an HR or ticketing integration next, one interface across 300+ connectors keeps your team from starting over.
  • Webhook reliability matters. A successful HTTP response is the start of the job, not the end of it. Relay Webhooks retries for up to 72 hours, and replayable logs plus a dead-letter queue mean an upstream outage doesn’t have to cost you records.
  • You debug by customer. Trace Console shows every request, retry and error per connection, with health scores and alerts when credentials break, so support doesn’t start with a log hunt.
  • You want predictable plans. Pricing is published, from Starter for prototypes to Team with all 300+ connectors.

Where Apideck may suit you better

Connectors differ, and so do platforms. If accounting and ERP are the only integrations on your roadmap, Apideck’s depth there is worth a look. If your security review requires that no third-party data is stored, its zero data retention approach is built for that. And if you would rather pay per active customer than by connected accounts and API calls, its pricing model may fit better.

Whichever you pick, decide on lifetime cost, not the upfront quote. Our build vs. buy checklist is a good place to start.
