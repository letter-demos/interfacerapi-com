Interfacer API vs Apideck

Both are unified APIs: you integrate once and your customers connect the tools they already use. Apideck leads with accounting and ERP. Interfacer API is built for the long tail of 300+ connectors and for keeping the boring parts reliable: credentials, retries and webhooks.

Talk to us

The short version

Choose Interfacer API if

  • Your customers use a wide spread of CRMs, accounting tools, ticketing systems and HR platforms, and you need breadth across 300+ connectors
  • You want retries with backoff, jitter and per-vendor rules, plus a dead-letter queue, handled for you
  • You need webhooks you can replay, including for APIs that have no webhooks of their own
  • You want typed SDKs for TypeScript, Python, Go and Ruby

Apideck may fit better if

  • Accounting and ERP are the heart of your product, and you want the 55+ accounting and ERP platforms Apideck lists
  • A policy of not storing third-party data on the vendor's infrastructure is a hard requirement (Apideck describes this as zero data retention)
  • You want a hosted, branded integrations marketplace page
  • You prefer pricing per active customer rather than per plan

Where the difference shows up

Credentials that stay healthy

Both products handle OAuth and token refresh, so neither leaves you writing refresh-token code. Our view is that expired or revoked credentials, not vendor API changes, are the real silent killer of integrations. Interfacer refreshes tokens ahead of expiry, scores the health of every connection and alerts you in Slack or email before your customer's data goes stale.

Retries with a plan for giving up

Flaky vendor APIs are a fact of life. Interfacer retries with exponential backoff, jitter and per-vendor rules, and when an event still won't land it goes to a dead-letter queue you can redeliver from in one click, rather than vanishing quietly.

Breadth versus depth

If accounting is your whole product, Apideck's accounting focus and use-case coverage (payables, receivables, bank feeds and more) is a real strength. If your customers each bring a different mix of CRM, ticketing, HR and accounting tools, Interfacer's 300+ connectors cover more of that long tail through one API.

What neither of us is

Interfacer API is not an end-user automation tool like Zapier, not a no-code workflow builder and not an iPaaS for internal enterprise systems. It is an API your engineers call from your product. And if an integration is your core differentiator, building it in-house can be the right call; for everything else, a unified API is usually cheaper.
