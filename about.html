About Interfacer API

Interfacer API started in 2021 when two backend engineers, Tomas Eklund and Priya Nandakumar, found themselves on the same unglamorous task at different companies: writing the fourteenth custom integration for a product that was supposed to be about something else. Every vendor had a different auth flow, a different idea of pagination, and a different way of failing at 2 a.m.

They built an internal tool to wrap that pain in a single interface, and friends at other startups kept asking to borrow it. By early 2022 they left their jobs, opened a small office in Minneapolis, and turned the tool into a hosted product.

Today a team of 23 maintains more than 300 connectors. We still read every integration bug report ourselves, because the most useful thing we can do is make sure the boring parts of integrations stay boring.

What we value

  • Make the boring parts reliable
  • Be honest about what each connector can and cannot do
  • Document everything, including our failures
  • Never lock customers in: your data and credentials are always exportable
