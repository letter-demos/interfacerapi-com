Where Interfacer API is different

Credential health comes first

Expired and revoked credentials quietly break more integrations than vendor API changes. Auth Vault refreshes tokens automatically, scores every connection's health, and alerts you before your customer notices stale data.

Delivery you can replay

Relay Webhooks retries with exponential backoff for up to 72 hours, then parks the event in a dead-letter queue. When an upstream vendor has a bad afternoon, you redeliver with one click.

Every request in one place

Trace Console shows each request, retry, and error across all of your customers' connections, with searchable logs and 30 days of history on the Team plan.

A smaller first bill

Starter is $49 a month and Team is $399 with all 300+ connectors, Relay Webhooks, and Trace Console included. See pricing for the limits on each plan.

When Unified may suit you better

We'd rather you pick the right tool than the wrong one twice. Unified is worth a close look if:

  • You need categories beyond CRM, accounting, ticketing, and HR, such as healthcare, legal practice management, or e-commerce.
  • You're building AI agents or copilots and want an MCP server on the same connections.
  • Your team works in PHP, Java, or C# and wants a first-party SDK.
  • You want every read to go live to the source API with no customer records stored at rest.
  • You connect many customers at low volume and prefer paying by API calls rather than by connected accounts.

When Interfacer API is the better fit

  • Your customers live in CRMs, accounting tools, ticketing systems, and HR platforms.
  • Broken syncs are showing up as support tickets, and you want credential problems flagged before customers find them.
  • You need webhook events you can retry, inspect, and replay, including from APIs that have no webhooks of their own.
  • You'd like to start small and grow into a plan, rather than start at $750 a month.
